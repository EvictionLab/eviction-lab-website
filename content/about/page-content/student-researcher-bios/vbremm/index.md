Katherine Lee is a junior at Princeton University majoring in Public and International Affairs with minors in Entrepreneurship and Global Health Policy. At the Eviction Lab, she has contributed to research on housing instability and currently examines tenant–landlord dynamics and eviction risk. She has also conducted research on political rhetoric and foreign lobbying in the U.S. and held international internships in South Korea, France, and Singapore at the intersection of healthcare technology, product development, and growth strategy. Her studies at Yonsei University and Cité Internationale Universitaire de Paris strengthened her cross-cultural perspective and commitment to global public health. Although engaged in several campus organizations, she finds her greatest fulfillment in side projects in marketing and business development with firms and startups—experiences she aims to leverage in launching her own venture to address health and social inequities.
