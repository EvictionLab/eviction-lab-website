Tas studied Molecular and Cell Biology at UC Berkeley before earning her master’s in Biomedical Informatics at the University of Chicago. She worked as a bioinformatics programmer in UCSF’s Hollenbach Lab before joining the Eviction Lab in 2022. As Program Coordinator, Tas handles the Eviction Lab’s daily operations and oversees its creative work across print, digital media, photography, and physical space. Her projects include presentations, educational resources, and fundraising materials. Tas grew up in Nairobi and now calls Princeton home. Outside the lab, she divides her time unevenly between sewing, crochet, painting, photography, and dog.
