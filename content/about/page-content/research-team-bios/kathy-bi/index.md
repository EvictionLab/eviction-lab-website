Kathy holds a PhD in Applied Economics from the University of Pennsylvania and a BA in Economics from Williams College. Her research focuses on housing instability among renters and the evaluation of policies aimed at reducing eviction and displacement. In earlier work, she examined how procedural aspects of eviction court can lead to high default judgment rates and disadvantage tenants. Kathy uses both experimental and quasi-experimental methods, collaborating with local governments and organizations to study the causes and consequences of housing insecurity and to generate causal evidence that informs policy.
