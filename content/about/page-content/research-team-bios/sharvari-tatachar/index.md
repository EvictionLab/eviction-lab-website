Sharvari is a Research Specialist at the Eviction Lab. She earned her B.A. in Computer Science and English Literature from Swarthmore College and is interested in pursuing a Ph.D. at the intersection of machine learning and fairness. At the Lab, she hopes to develop machine-learning tools for studying housing affordability, rental markets, and displacement. Before joining the Eviction Lab, Sharvari was an A3D3 Post-Baccalaureate Research Fellow. She collaborated with the Laser Interferometer Gravitational-Wave Observatory (LIGO) and Georgia Tech’s Graph Computation and Machine Learning Lab to develop methods for automating gravitational-wave denoising. At Swarthmore, she studied the societal impact of deepfakes with the Collective Resilience Lab and served as a managing editor of the campus newspaper. Sharvari is originally from Princeton and is excited to be back within walking distance of the Bent Spoon. Outside of work, she enjoys street and film photography, making music with friends, and trying new arts and crafts.
