Krystal (Cheongeui) is a Research Specialist at the Eviction Lab. She earned her bachelor’s degree in Journalism from the University of Texas at Austin in 2019 and completed her master’s in urban spatial analytics at the University of Pennsylvania. Much of her graduate work involved transforming complex spatial data into compelling narratives that expose hidden inequalities within housing and urban planning systems. Krystal loves diving deep into datasets and discovering needles in the haystack—uncovering patterns in hopes of driving meaningful policy change. Her research interests extend to public health and social equity. When she’s not with her laptop, you’ll find her taking walks with her camera, dancing, or hunting for vinyl records.
