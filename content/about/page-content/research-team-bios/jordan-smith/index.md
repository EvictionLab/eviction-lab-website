Jordan Smith is a Research Specialist at the Eviction Lab where he works on projects centered around homelessness with a particular focus on synthesizing HMIS (Homeless Management Information System) data. After receiving his B.A. in Political Science and M.S. in Public Policy from Saint Joseph's University, Jordan worked as a Program Analyst and Continuum of Care Team Co-Lead back home in Essex County, New Jersey. He hopes to utilize his experience working within a CoC to assist CoCs across the country as they work toward improving their service delivery systems. Jordan enjoys reading, collecting old video games and supporting Celtic Football Club.
