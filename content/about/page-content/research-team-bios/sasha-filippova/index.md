Sasha joined the Eviction Lab as a Data Engineer in September 2025. Before joining the team, she worked at New York City’s Department of City Planning as part of the Data Engineering team. She co-developed the data product that powers the Green Fast Track app, which supports the city’s sustainability goals by streamlining eligibility checks and permitting. She also led a quality-improvement effort for PLUTO, improving the reliability of one of the city’s most widely used land use datasets.

Sasha cares about transparent, high-quality data that strengthens public policy. She enjoys working with open-source tools and regularly shares her work, presenting at public conferences and internal DCP events. Her background includes roles in local government and the nonprofit sector. Outside of work, she enjoys reading, traveling, and hiking.
