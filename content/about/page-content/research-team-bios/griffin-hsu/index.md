Griffin Hsu is a Research Specialist at the Eviction Lab, where he works on projects examining rental prices and eviction filing fees. He earned his B.A. from the University of Oregon in 2025, with a double major in economics and data science. His undergraduate thesis examined how the 2018 U.S.–China trade war affected county-level housing prices. As an undergraduate, Griffin worked as a research assistant for faculty across campus and built basketball attendance forecasting models for the university's business school. His research interests center on housing markets and predictive indicators of rental prices. In his free time, he enjoys basketball, hiking, and board and card games.
