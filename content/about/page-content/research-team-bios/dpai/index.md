Dash Pai is a Research Specialist at the Eviction Lab where he works on projects related to child welfare policy and the causes of eviction. He received an A.B. in sociology from Princeton University, where he also worked as an Eviction Lab research assistant and as an occasional okra farmer with The Seed Farm. His senior thesis examined the relationship between municipal zoning policy and class segregation in U.S. cities and was awarded the Isidore Brown Thesis Prize. Dash enjoys puzzles, eating his way through New Jersey and New York, and all things maps.
