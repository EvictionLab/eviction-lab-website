Katie Weinstein is the Administrative Assistant at the Eviction Lab. She holds a Bachelor of Fine Arts from the University of North Carolina School of the Arts.

Prior to joining the Eviction Lab, Katie worked at Princeton’s Broadcast Center, where she helped produce videos documenting the innovative research across campus. Her background in the arts involved working with a variety of organizations, such as Lincoln Center Education, McCarter Theatre Center, Columbia University, and others.

Katie is thrilled to bring her love of spreadsheets and schedules to the Eviction Lab. She deeply believes housing is a human right and is honored to contribute her experience to support the Lab’s mission.

In her free time, Katie also advocates for a more equitable entertainment industry through the production company she co-founded, Raw Daisies.
