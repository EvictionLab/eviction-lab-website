Jun (Jennifer) Luu is a Research Specialist at the Eviction Lab. Her interest in housing led her first to the construction side, where she spent three years as a construction engineer on NYCHA RAD conversions in New York City and on medical centers in Los Angeles and Chicago before turning to research. She holds a Master of Urban Spatial Analytics from the University of Pennsylvania, where she wrote her thesis, "Mapping New Vocabularies of Neighborhood Change: An Inductive Text Analysis using Real Estate Listings." She earned her B.S. in Construction Engineering and Management from Virginia Tech, in her home state, where her work focused on reducing housing construction costs through material choices and MEP systems.Her research interests include housing, homelessness, and migration. Outside of work, Jun spends her time with her dog, Hendrix, going to live music events, and sleeping in excess.
